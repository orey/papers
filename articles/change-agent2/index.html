    Change agent2

    Hello. I want to write a paper about digital transformation and why those transformations are complicated. The title could be: Digital transformation starts with the change agent transformation

    Here is the synopsis.

    Note

    We are talking about transformation in which people will not be fired.

    1. The starting point of the digital transformation: conscience of inefficiency

    When there is a need for digital transformation, that means that the management realized that there was a problem, often an efficiency problem. That is not always the case for the organization that is targeted by the transformation. Most of the members may realize it is complicated, but they are doing the job.

    1. We transform something that works badly but that works

    Digital transformation comes where there is money to finance it. Companies finance the optimization of processes through digital tools. That means that you digitally transform something that is working. That means that, however inefficient, the organization gets the job done. That means that digital transformation can break what is working, which explains transformation comes with risks.

    1. Organization self-transformation is (almost) impossible

    Most of the time, the targeted organization has not the skills to transform itself. Many people know how to execute processes but few know how to change them for the better. Members of that organization may also be afraid to loose their jobs if the organization is optimized. Being lack of skills or fear, the targeted organization will resist change. So change must come from the outside.

    1. Resistance is the standard behavior

    The members of the targeted organization are experts in their inefficient processes, that's why they get the job done. This expertise, however not usable outside of the organization provides a leverage for members towards any change agent that would come from outside of the organization.

    1. Change agents that don't adapt

    The change agents most of the time come from the outside of the organization. They mostly come with patterns, sometimes with experience, rarely with a listen and learn phase. The more transformation patterns they want to apply, the greater the resistance.

    1. Listen and learn

    The change agents must, at some point, become experts in the content to be able to change, or even to specify the tools for change. They must acquire a deep knowledge of the dysfunctional landscape and start to transform. This is the listen and learn phase for the change agent. Indeed, in that phase, the change agent changes itself. It must stay modest face to a dysfunctional or inefficient organization that gets the job done.

    1. Experience needed

    When the change agents talk the local language and know the local traditions, they can use their experience to implement the change in an adapted way. They get can get inspired by transformation patterns but must be very cautious about the local specificity.

    Conclusion

    In the digital transformation, the first to change is the change agent. Then the organization can be changed. The change agent must never forget that, however inefficient, costly, chaotic, the organization to transform usually gets the job done. If the change agent comes with its certitudes, transformation patterns and diagnosis, even if it is right, the change may be impossible.

    Could you elaborate a bit on this plan to make an article? Can you respect the order and logic of the various points? Once you have the article, I am OK to have your comments about the content, what do you think is important and if I forgot some elements that you would find important.
